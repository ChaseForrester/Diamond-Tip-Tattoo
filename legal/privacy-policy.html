1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to the website www.diamondtiptattoo.com.au, related booking tools, Facebook/Instagram/Messenger communications, email, phone, in-studio forms, and any app or page operated by Diamond Tip Tattoo in Dapto, New South Wales, Australia.

We are a private, appointment-focused tattoo and body-piercing studio. For privacy requests, email hello@diamondtiptattoo.com with the subject line “Privacy request”.

Where the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) apply to us, we aim to handle personal information in a way that is consistent with those principles. Small-business exemptions may apply in some cases; we still aim to meet the standards below as good practice and for platform compliance (including Meta).

3. Why we use your information

We use personal information to:

  • Respond to consultation and booking requests
  • Design, schedule and deliver tattoo or piercing services
  • Send appointment reminders, deposits and aftercare information
  • Process studio shop / pickup orders
  • Improve the website and customer experience
  • Comply with law (e.g. age checks, health & safety records where required)
  • Defend legal claims and maintain studio records
  • With your consent — market the studio using photos/videos of your tattoo or session
  • Operate Facebook/Instagram Page messaging and related business tools

We rely on one or more of: your consent; steps needed to provide a service you requested; our legitimate interests in running a studio (balanced against your rights); and legal obligations.

4. Meta, Facebook, Instagram & Messenger

We use Meta platforms (Facebook, Instagram, Messenger) to talk with clients and, where configured, to receive website form notifications and ads insights.

How data can flow

1
You submit a website form, message our Page, or interact with our ads/content.
2
Details may be stored in our CRM (e.g. Firebase), email tools, and/or Messenger inbox.
3
Our systems (including secure server functions) may send a summary to the studio via Messenger using Meta’s APIs.
4
Meta processes data under its own terms as a platform provider. See Meta’s policies linked below.

What we may process via Meta

  • Messages you send to our Facebook Page / Instagram account
  • Your public profile name and Page-scoped ID when you message us
  • Form summaries our systems deliver into Messenger for studio staff
  • If Meta Pixel / Conversions tools are enabled: events such as page views, booking leads, or purchases (configured by us)

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your Messenger content to data brokers
  • We do not use Messenger content to train unrelated public AI products for sale
  • We only message you on Messenger in line with Meta’s rules (e.g. after you message us, or within allowed windows/tags)

9. Your rights (Australia)

Subject to applicable law, you may request to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Withdraw consent (e.g. marketing or media use) where processing is consent-based
  • Ask us to delete information we no longer need (we may retain some records where legally required)
  • Complain about our handling of your information

Contact: hello@diamondtiptattoo.com. We aim to respond within a reasonable time (generally within 30 days).

You may also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC): oaic.gov.au · 1300 363 992

10. Children & age restrictions

Tattoo services at our studio are for people 18 years or older with valid photo ID. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for tattoo bookings.

Piercing age rules and ID requirements are explained at booking and must be followed. Parents/guardians should contact us before submitting a minor’s details.

11. Health & sensitive information

Tattooing and piercing can involve limited health-related information you choose to give us (e.g. allergies, skin conditions, medications, pregnancy). We use this only for safety and service quality.

Please do not send highly sensitive medical records through public Messenger threads if avoidable — ask us for a safer method if needed.

12. Photos, video & marketing use

We may photograph or film healed work or sessions only with your permission (written media release or clear documented consent).

Use the dedicated form: Social Media & Marketing Release.

You can refuse marketing use and still receive tattoo services. Refusing does not affect consumer guarantees under the ACL for the service itself.

13. Australian Consumer Law (ACCC) & tattoo services

Under the Australian Consumer Law (ACL), services generally come with consumer guarantees, including that services will be provided with due care and skill, be fit for the purpose made known, and be supplied within a reasonable time when no time is set.

How this interacts with deposits, cancellations and touch-ups is explained in our:

  • Terms & Conditions
  • Refund Policy

Nothing in our policies is intended to exclude, restrict or modify rights that cannot be excluded under the ACL. Where a failure is major, remedies under the ACL may include refund, further service, or other remedies as applicable.

Helpful official resources:

  • ACCC — Consumer rights & guarantees
  • ACCC — Unfair contract terms

We discuss risks and aftercare before work. Custom design work and time-based sessions are services — clear quotes and deposit terms will be confirmed before you pay a deposit.
