HEALING GUIDE

Tattoo aftercare basics

Your artist will give personalised instructions. As a general guide:

  1. Keep it clean — wash gently with mild soap, pat dry. Avoid scrubbing.
  2. Moisturise lightly — thin layer of recommended ointment/cream. Don’t over-moisturise.
  3. Don’t pick — let scabs/flakes fall naturally.
  4. Sun & soak — avoid pools, ocean, saunas and direct sun while healing.
  5. Loose clothing — reduce friction on the area.

Shop studio-approved aftercare in our Studio Shop. Questions? Call (02) 4261 4311 or book a follow-up.
