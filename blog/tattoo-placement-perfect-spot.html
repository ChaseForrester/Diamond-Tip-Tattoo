Tattoo Placements: Finding the Perfect Spot

Share Facebook X / Twitter WhatsApp LinkedIn Email

Tattoo placement can make or break a design. Fine line florals and script look stunning on wrists, collarbones, ribs and ankles. Large realism, portraits and black & grey sleeves need bigger canvases — upper arm, thigh, back or full sleeve.

At Diamond Tip Tattooing on Baan Baan Street, Dapto, we design for how your body moves. A piece that wraps the forearm differently to a flat chest panel. Cover-ups need enough solid area and strategic flow so the old work disappears into the new story.

Nervous about visibility for work? We plan discreet placements that still look intentional. Want something bold for Illawarra summers? We’ll talk sun exposure and healing too.

Book a free consultation — bring reference photos and we’ll sketch placement options before any deposit.
