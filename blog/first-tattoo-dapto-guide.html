Getting Your First Tattoo in Dapto: A Calm, Honest Guide

Share Facebook X / Twitter WhatsApp LinkedIn Email

Your first tattoo should feel exciting, not intimidating. Diamond Tip is appointment-only: private, no walk-in crowds, time to get the design right.

Start with a free consultation. Tell us your idea, budget and any nerves. We’ll suggest size, style (fine line, realism, black & grey, traditional) and a realistic session plan. Small pieces can be under an hour; larger work may need multiple sessions.

Pain is personal. Outer arms and calves are usually easier; ribs and feet tend to sting more. We take breaks if you need them. Bring ID — you must be 18+.

Eat beforehand, hydrate, wear clothes that give easy access to the area. After the session you leave with clear aftercare and a healed-result focus — not a rushed stencil and goodbye.
